01 · About

Every day, millions of listeners press play on the products I help build.

I'm a Senior Product Manager based in Paris, currently leading product strategy for Radio France's website and mobile app, serving over 10 million users and generating 103 million monthly streams. These are all-time audience records for the group.

Over the past six years, I've shipped consumer products at scale across France, the UK, and Canada. From the redesign of the Radio France mobile app to the digital subscription launch for Le Journal du Dimanche and Paris Match, my work is grounded in user-centered discovery, data-driven decisions, and tight cross-functional execution.

I also teach Product Management as a guest lecturer at EFREI, YNOV, and IIM in Paris.

Outside product: former semi-professional goalkeeper (Paris FC and Paris Saint-Germain academies, professional contract at Bangor City FC), FIFA-licensed agent, and long-distance runner training for the Six Super Half Marathons.

02 · Selected work
01
Mobile · 2024

Redesigning the Radio France mobile app from the ground up.

Radio France · Released August 2024

Led the end-to-end redesign of the Radio France mobile app, the listening companion for 7 stations and millions of daily users. The work spanned product strategy, navigation rethink, listening experience, and store positioning, alongside the rollout of a Backend-for-Frontend architecture co-designed with the API team.

The redesign reframed listening as a daily ritual rather than a list of stations, and rebuilt the foundations to scale across web and mobile from one shared design language.

Web · 2026

Unifying web and app on a single design system.

Radio France · Released March 2026

Led the redesign of the Radio France website on a unified design system shared with the mobile app, one visual language, one component library, one experience across surfaces. The goal was to remove the cognitive cost of switching between web and mobile for the same listener.

The launch leveraged the same BFF architecture deployed for the app, accelerating delivery and creating a foundation for future product work across the entire ecosystem.

03
Subscription · 2019—2021

Building the first digital subscription product for two iconic news brands.

Lagardère · Le Journal du Dimanche & Paris Match

Designed and launched the first end-to-end digital subscription product for Le Journal du Dimanche and Paris Match: pricing tiers, paywall logic, conversion funnels, and the full path from free reader to paying subscriber.

The internal narrative was that print readers wouldn't pay digitally. The data from comparable European publishers said otherwise. The gap wasn't appetite, it was the absence of a credible product offer.
